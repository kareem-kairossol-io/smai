Frequently Asked Questions

General Questions

What is a wellness clinic?

A wellness clinic focuses on preventive, therapeutic, and performance-based care. We combine medical expertise with personalized treatment plans to support your health, recovery, and appearance — all under professional medical supervision.

Do I need a consultation before starting treatment?

Yes. Every client undergoes an initial medical consultation to assess health history, goals, and suitability for any treatment. This ensures safety, effectiveness, and personalized care.

Are treatments medically supervised?

Absolutely. All services are delivered or supervised by licensed physicians and certified healthcare professionals

Clinical Nutrition

What is clinical nutrition?

Clinical nutrition uses evidence-based dietary plans to manage medical conditions, optimize body composition, improve metabolism, and enhance athletic performance.

Is nutrition only for weight loss?

Not at all. Our nutrition programs support:
  • Weight management
  • Muscle gain & fat loss
  • Metabolic health
  • Sports performance
  • Recovery and injury prevention
  • Hormonal balance

Do athletes need a different nutrition plan?

Yes. Athletes require specialized performance nutrition plans tailored to training load, competition schedule, recovery needs, and body composition goal

IV Drips & Medical Wellness

What are IV drips?

IV drips deliver fluids, vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants directly into the bloodstream for faster and more effective absorption.

What are the benefits of IV therapy?

IV therapy can help with:
  • Energy and immunity support
  • Hydration and recovery
  • Muscle fatigue and soreness
  • Stress and burnout
  • Skin and hair health

Is IV therapy safe?

Yes, when administered after medical assessment and under physician supervision. All formulations are medically approved and customized to your needs

Recovery & Physiotherapy

Who needs physiotherapy?

Physiotherapy is beneficial for:

  • Chronic pain sufferers

What recovery services do you offer for athletes?

Our recovery programs may include:

  • Muscle recovery techniques
  • Injury prevention programs

Do I need imaging or a referral?

Not always. Our physiotherapists perform full assessments. If imaging or referrals are required, we will guide you

Laser Hair Removal – Candela FAQ

What is laser hair removal?

Laser hair removal is a medical aesthetic procedure that uses focused light energy to target hair follicles, reducing unwanted hair growth safely and effectively.

What is the Candela laser machine?

Candela is a world-leading, FDA-approved laser technology used globally in medical and aesthetic clinics. It is known for its high effectiveness, safety profile, and suitability for different skin types, including darker skin tones.

Why is Candela considered one of the best laser machines?

Candela machines offer:
  • Advanced cooling technology for comfort
  • High precision targeting of hair follicles
  • Safe treatment for multiple skin types
  • Faster sessions with long-lasting results

How does laser hair removal work?

The laser targets melanin in the hair follicle. The heat damages the follicle, slowing down or preventing future hair growth without harming surrounding skin.

How many sessions will I need?

Most clients require 6–8 sessions, spaced 4–6 weeks apart, depending on:
  • Hair thickness and colour
  • Skin type
  • Treatment area
  • Hormonal factors

When will I start seeing results?

You may notice reduced hair growth after the first 1–2 sessions, with progressively smoother skin after each treatment.

Is laser hair removal permanent?

Laser hair removal leads to long-term hair reduction. Some fine regrowth may occur over time, which can be managed with maintenance sessions.

Is laser hair removal safe?

Yes. When performed using FDA-approved Candela machines by trained professionals, laser hair removal is safe and effective.

Is the treatment painful?

Most clients describe the sensation as mild snapping or warmth. Candela’s built-in cooling system significantly reduces discomfort.

Can Candela be used on dark or sensitive skin?

Yes. Candela technology is designed to safely treat a wide range of skin tones, including darker and sensitive skin, when properly assessed.

Do I need to prepare before my session?

Before treatment:
  • Avoid waxing or plucking for 3–4 weeks
  • Shave the area 24 hours before the session
  • Avoid sun exposure and tanning

What should I avoid after treatment?

  • Avoid sun exposure and heat for 48 hours
  • Use sunscreen on treated areas
  • Avoid hot showers, saunas, and intense workouts for 24–48 hours

Is there downtime?

No. Most clients return to normal activities immediately after treatment

Who can undergo laser hair removal?

Most adults are suitable candidates. A consultation is required to assess skin type, hair color, medical history, and hormonal factors.

Is laser hair removal safe for men?

Absolutely. Laser hair removal is effective for both women and men on areas such as face, chest, back, arms, and legs.

Can I do laser hair removal if I have hormonal issues?

You can, but results may vary. Our medical team will advise you on expectations and treatment plans.

Do I need a consultation before starting?

Yes. A consultation ensures:
  • Correct machine settings
  • Skin and hair assessment
  • Safe and personalized treatment plan

Who performs the treatment?

All treatments are performed by trained laser specialists under medical supervision, following strict safety protocols.
