TECAR THERAPY = Transfer of Energy Capacitive and Resistive.
It is a form of radiofrequency diathermy used in physical medicine and rehabilitation. 
Depending on the mode and parameters, it can produce controlled deep tissue heating and/or non-thermal effects. 
The proposed therapeutic effects include:
Increased local blood flow and tissue perfusion
Reduction of muscle tension and spasm
Pain modulation
Improved tissue mobility
Support of tissue recovery
Improvement of range of motion
Preparation of tissues before therapeutic exercise or manual therapy
TECAR Therapy for Sports Injuries & RecoveryAn advanced radiofrequency therapy used as part of sports rehabilitation programs to help manage muscle and joint pain, muscle strains, tendon and ligament injuries, stiffness, and movement limitations. 
TECAR therapy can support local circulation, reduce muscle tension, improve mobility, and prepare tissues for therapeutic exercise and rehabilitation. 
At SMAI Wellness Clinics, it is integrated into individualized treatment plans according to the athlete’s condition, injury stage, and recovery goals

D. Aesthetic Medicine Services
Natural Results. Medical Precision.
At SMAI, we believe that effective aesthetic care begins with a thorough medical assessment. 
When appropriate, dermatology medicine is recommended as a preliminary step to diagnose and manage underlying skin or hair conditions before proceeding with cosmetic treatments. 
This approach helps ensure that aesthetic procedures are safe, appropriate, and tailored to each individual’s needs.
Our Services:
Botox is a minimally invasive injectable treatment that temporarily relaxes targeted facial muscles, helping reduce the appearance of wrinkles and fine lines. It can create a smoother, more refreshed appearance while maintaining natural facial expression when appropriately administered.
Recommended for:Individuals concerned about expression lines such as forehead lines, frown lines, and crow’s feet, or those seeking a smoother and more refreshed facial appearance.
Dermal Fillers are injectable treatments containing hyaluronic acid and other biocompatible materials, used to restore volume, enhance facial contours, and soften the appearance of selected lines and folds. 
Recommended for:Individuals seeking facial volume restoration, enhanced contours, improved symmetry, or reduction of the appearance of certain lines and folds.
Derma pen is a microneedling treatment that uses fine, controlled micro-needles to stimulate the skin’s natural renewal processes and support collagen production. It can improve skin texture, smoothness, and overall appearance, with minimal downtime.
Recommended for:Individuals concerned about acne scars, enlarged pores, uneven skin texture, fine lines, or dull-looking skin, and those seeking overall skin rejuvenation
HydraFacial is a non-invasive skin treatment that deeply cleanses, exfoliates, extracts impurities, and hydrates the skin. It helps improve skin texture, clarity, and radiance with minimal downtime.
Recommended for:
Individuals with dull, dehydrated, congested or uneven-looking skin, enlarged pores, excess oil, or mild surface impurities, as well as those seeking deep cleansing, hydration and a fresher, more radiant complexion.
Plasma Separation and Reinjection
Plasma separation and reinjection is a personalized treatment that uses the patient’s own plasma to support skin rejuvenation and tissue repair. It may help improve skin quality, hydration, and overall appearance.
Recommended for:
Individuals seeking improvement in skin texture, fine lines, dullness and overall skin quality, particularly those interested in regenerative and minimally invasive aesthetic treatments.
Hair-Loss Injection Treatment
Hair-loss injection treatment delivers targeted nutrients and therapeutic ingredients directly into the scalp to support hair follicles and reduce hair shedding. 
Recommended for:
Individuals want to improve hair density and strength as part of a personalized hair-loss treatment plan
Glass Skin Booster is a facial injectable treatment containing hyaluronic acid and a blend of skin-supporting nutrients. It helps improve skin hydration, radiance, smoothness, and overall skin quality, giving the complexion a fresher, healthier, and more luminous appearance.
Recommended for:Individuals with dehydrated, dull, or tired-looking skin, uneven texture, fine lines, or those seeking a more hydrated, radiant, and healthy-looking complexion
