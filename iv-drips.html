IV Drips & Medical Wellness

Targeted Therapy. Immediate Support.

Our IV therapies deliver essential vitamins, minerals, and hydration directly into the bloodstream for maximum absorption and faster results.

1. NAD+ IV Therapy

Cellular Energy, Vitality & Healthy Aging

NAD+ IV Therapy is designed to support cellular energy production and overall vitality. Delivered intravenously, NAD+ provides a direct route of administration and may be incorporated into personalized wellness programs focused on energy, cellular function, and healthy aging.

Recommended For

Adults experiencing low energy or looking to support overall vitality, cellular energy and wellness as part of a personalized health program.

2. Energy & Vitality

Targeted Nutritional Support for Energy Metabolism

The Energy & Vitality IV is designed to support the body when you feel physically or mentally depleted. A carefully selected combination of vitamins, minerals, and nutrients provides targeted nutritional support to help maintain energy metabolism, hydration, and overall vitality.

Recommended For

Individuals experiencing fatigue or reduced activity due to demanding work, busy lifestyles, physical or mental exertion, or inadequate nutritional intake.

3. Anti-Stress & Relaxation

Nervous-System Support & Balanced Wellbeing

The Anti-Stress & Relaxation IV is formulated to provide nutritional support during periods of stress, fatigue, and demanding lifestyles. A tailored blend of vitamins and essential nutrients supports normal nervous-system function and overall wellbeing, helping you take a moment to recharge and restore balance. It can be incorporated into a personalized wellness plan according to your individual needs.

Recommended For

Adults experiencing work-related pressure, daily stress, mental fatigue, or demanding routines who are looking for nutritional support as part of a comprehensive wellness program.

4. Immunity Support

Natural Defence Mechanisms & Antioxidant Protection

The Immunity Support IV provides a concentrated combination of vitamins, minerals, and antioxidants selected to support normal immune function and the body’s natural defence mechanisms. It is designed as part of a proactive wellness approach, particularly during periods of increased physical or environmental stress, while supporting overall nutritional status and wellbeing.

Recommended For

Individuals looking to support their immune function and nutritional status, particularly during periods of physical or environmental stress or when dietary intake may be inadequate.

5. Detox & Liver Support

Nutritional & Antioxidant Support for Natural Detoxification

The Detox & Liver Support IV is designed to provide nutritional and antioxidant support for the body’s natural detoxification processes. Its formulation may include nutrients involved in antioxidant defence and normal liver function, helping support the body’s own mechanisms for processing and eliminating metabolic by-products.

Recommended For

Adults looking for nutritional and antioxidant support as part of a healthy lifestyle, particularly during periods of demanding or irregular routines.

6. Hair Boost

Targeted Nutrients for Follicle Strength & Vitality

Provides targeted vitamins, minerals, and nutrients to support healthy hair, strengthen hair follicles, and promote overall hair health. It can be used as a supportive treatment for hair loss and increased hair shedding, as part of a personalized hair-care and medical treatment plan.

Recommended For

Individuals experiencing hair loss, weak or thinning hair, increased hair shedding and those looking for additional nutritional support as part of comprehensive hair loss treatment plan.

7. Beauty & Glow

Skin Health, Deep Hydration & Inner Radiance

The Beauty & Glow IV is designed to support skin health, hydration, and overall radiance from within. It complements a comprehensive skincare and wellness routine, helping support a fresh, healthy-looking appearance.

Recommended For

Adults interested in supporting skin health, hydration, nutritional status, and overall skin appearance as part of a comprehensive beauty and wellness routine.

8. Weight Management

Medically Supervised Support for Metabolic Wellness

The Weight Management IV is designed to complement a medically supervised approach to healthy weight management. Depending on individual needs, selected vitamins, minerals, and nutrients may help support energy metabolism and overall nutritional status.

Recommended For

Individuals participating in a medical weight-management program who may require additional hydration or nutritional support based on their assessment.

9. Post-Bariatric Support

Targeted Nutritional Support Following Bariatric Procedures

The Post-Bariatric IV is designed to provide targeted nutritional support for individuals following bariatric surgery. After bariatric procedures, changes in digestion and nutrient absorption may increase the risk of certain nutritional deficiencies. The formulation should therefore be selected according to the individual’s needs.

Recommended For

Individuals who have undergone bariatric surgery and require additional nutritional support based on their medical assessment, laboratory results, dietary intake, or specific nutritional deficiencies.

10. Athlete Recovery

Electrolytes, Muscle Recovery & Performance Sustainability

The Athlete Recovery IV is designed to support hydration and nutritional recovery. A selected combination of fluids, electrolytes, vitamins, and other nutrients may help support normal hydration, energy metabolism, and recovery needs. This IV therapy assists in restoring essential nutrients required for muscle function, recovery, and performance sustainability.

Recommended For

Athletes and individuals undertaking intensive training or competition who may require additional hydration and nutritional support following physical exertion.

11. Royal Wellness IV Drip

Where Medical Excellence Meets Luxury Wellness

Comprehensive IV Support for Total Body Wellness: The Royal Wellness Drip is our signature, all-inclusive IV therapy designed to support overall health, balance, and optimal body function.

Recommended For

Individuals seeking complete wellness support, preventative care, lifestyle optimization, or a balanced IV therapy covering multiple wellness needs.

All IV treatments are administered by licensed medical professionals following a full health assessment.
